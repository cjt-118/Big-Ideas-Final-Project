## ** Project Description**

To determine the **"best" neighborhood in Pittsburgh**, our group analyzed four different metrics:

- **Economic Advantage Score** – Measures aggregate household income from the last 12 months (ACS 2014 data).  
- **Non-Traffic Citations** – Counts non-traffic citations issued per neighborhood.  
- **Fire Incidents** – Reports fire-related incidents across various blocks in the city.  
- **Crime Data** – Tracks violent crimes per 1,000 residents in each neighborhood.

By comparing these metrics across neighborhoods, we aimed to identify which areas demonstrate the strongest combination of safety, stability, and economic opportunity.
